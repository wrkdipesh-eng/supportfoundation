## 2. Understand Support Coordination Levels (Group 0132)

Support Coordination helps participants connect with mainstream services, allied health professionals, and daily support workers. When selecting a coordinator, ensure they offer the exact level required by your plan:

- **Level 1 (Support Connection):** Short-term assistance to build your confidence in managing informal and community supports.
- **Level 2 (Coordination of Supports):** Medium-term support to navigate complex service agreements and optimize plan funding.
- **Level 3 (Specialist Support Coordination):** Expert case management for participants facing high-complexity challenges or crisis situations.

To learn more about tailored coordination services in Roselands and Greater Sydney, visit [ReactCorp Support Coordination Services](https://reactcorpdisability.com.au/our-services/).

## 3. Prioritize 24/7 Emergency Response & SIL Accommodation

When unexpected challenges or crisis situations arise, standard 9-to-5 support is not enough. High-quality providers offer **24/7 active and emergency intake**, allowing participants to access urgent accommodation or support worker care in minutes.

If you or a loved one are looking for Supported Independent Living (SIL), ensure the provider offers:
- Thoughtful and compatible housemate matching
- Daily living and life skills development
- Active overnight supervision and personal care
- Transparent pricing aligned with the official [NDIS Price Guide](https://reactcorpdisability.com.au/price/)

## 4. Ensure Transparent & Compliant Pricing

Every dollar of your NDIS budget should translate into direct, meaningful support. Reputable providers adhere 100% to NDIA price caps without adding hidden administration fees or surprise markups.

Working with a provider who offers clear, itemized invoicing gives you complete peace of mind and ensures maximum care hours throughout your plan cycle.
